Air Swipe Slides

Present your slides and change them with a wave of the hand. The laptop webcam watches for your gestures. Nothing is uploaded: the slides and the camera stay on this computer.

2. The gestures

⟵ ✋
Right hand, palm to the camera, sweep left: nextShow the palm of your right hand and sweep it to your left, like turning a page forward.
🤚 ⟶
Left hand, palm to the camera, sweep right: previousShow the palm of your left hand and sweep it to your right. Only this goes back, so slides can't slip backwards by accident. Up and down movements are ignored.
🙌 ↔
Two hands apart: zoom inRaise both hands and pull them apart to zoom into that part of the slide. Push them together to zoom out. Clap to return to full view.
🤏
Pinch and drag: move aroundWhile zoomed, pinch thumb and index finger together and drag to move to another part of the slide. Swipes pause until you zoom back out.
✊ → 🖐
Fist, then open hand: throwGrab the slide with a fist (it shrinks and glows), then open your hand towards the room. It lands on participants' phones. Needs step 4 below.

Stand 1 to 3 metres from the laptop camera, in good light. Keyboard still works: ← → to change slides, B to pause, G to turn gestures on or off, S to turn the sound on or off, Z to turn hand zoom on or off, T to change the transition, H to check your hands again, + and − to zoom, R to reset zoom, C to hide the camera preview.

Using PowerPoint itself?

Download Air Swipe for PowerPoint, a small Windows app that turns the same gestures into key presses for PowerPoint, Google Slides or any slideshow, so animations and videos keep working.

Download for Windows (about 120 MB)
  1. Double-click AirSwipe.exe. No installation needed.
  2. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway (first time only).
  3. Start your PowerPoint slide show, then click on it once so it receives the gestures.
  4. Press F9 any time to turn gestures on or off.

The hand-tracking model downloads once (about 8 MB) when you start.
